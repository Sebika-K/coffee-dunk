Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Coffee Dunk is a friends-only coffee journal. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. Questions? Email sebikakhulal07@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and password (the password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see it), your username, and, if you add them, a profile photo and bio.
  • Your coffee posts: photos you upload, drink details, ratings, tasting notes, recipes, the café a post is for, and when you posted or edited it.
  • Friends and activity: friend requests and friendships, likes, saved posts, and people you've blocked.
  • Reports: if you report a post or a person, we store who reported, who or what was reported, the reason, and when.
  • Location (only if you allow it): when you tap "Near me", or when suggesting cafés while you post, your phone's approximate location is sent to our server to find nearby cafés. We don't store it.
  • Searches: city and café names you type are sent to our server to find results. Your recent searches are saved only on your phone.
  • Technical logs: our server's hosting provider keeps standard request logs (such as IP address and time) for security and troubleshooting.

We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we never sell your data.

How we use it

  • To run the app: your account, your coffee diary, your friends feed, likes and saves.
  • To find cafés and show café details, such as the address, hours and photos.
  • To show "What to order here" on café pages: drink ratings from everyone's posts at a café are combined into anonymous totals. Your name and posts are never shown to people who aren't your friends.
  • To suggest cafés based on what you and your friends have enjoyed.
  • To keep the community safe: to review reports and act on them.

Who can see what

  • Your posts are visible only to you and to people you've accepted as friends.
  • Your profile (username, photo and bio) can be seen by other signed-in Coffee Dunk users, so friends can find you.
  • Likes are private: only the person who posted sees who liked it.
  • Blocking someone removes the friendship and stops friend requests between you.

Services we rely on

We use these providers to run Coffee Dunk. They process data only to provide their service to us, under their own privacy and security commitments:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore database, Cloud Storage for photos, Hosting): stores your account, posts and photos.
  • Google Cloud Run: runs our server.
  • Google Maps Platform (Places): café search, suggestions and details. Search text, and location when you allow it, are sent to Google to get results.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • We keep your data while your account exists.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, posts, photos, likes, saves, friendships, blocks and username.
  • Reports you made or that were made about you may be kept for as long as needed to handle them and keep the community safe.
  • You can turn off location access at any time in your phone's Settings.
  • For any other request about your data, email us at the address above.

Children

Coffee Dunk is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. Big changes will also be announced in the app.
